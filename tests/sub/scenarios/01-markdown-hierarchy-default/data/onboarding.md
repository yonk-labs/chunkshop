# Onboarding

## Accounts
New hires receive credentials on day one. SSO is the default; request a local
account only for service integrations. Rotate your initial password within 48
hours of first login, and enroll a hardware token before the end of week one.

## Hardware
Laptops ship configured with the standard image. Peripherals can be ordered
from the internal catalog. Report damaged equipment within two business days
of noticing the issue; loaner machines are available at the front desk.

## First week
Shadow a peer for three days. Your manager will schedule a one-on-one at the
end of the week to review outstanding questions and unblock any paperwork.
