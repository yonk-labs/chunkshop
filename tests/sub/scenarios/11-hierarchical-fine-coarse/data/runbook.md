# Incident response

An incident is any user-impacting event lasting more than 5 minutes or
affecting more than 1% of traffic. Declare an incident immediately via
the incident channel; do not wait to confirm scope. The first responder
becomes the incident commander until explicitly handing off. The IC
drives updates and decisions; other responders take assigned tasks only.

# Post-mortems

Post-mortems are blameless and mandatory for every incident of severity
SEV-2 or higher. Draft within 48 hours of resolution. The IC owns the
draft; the on-call team reviews within a week. Publish to the engineering
all-hands before the monthly review. Track action items in the standing
incident board; don't leave them in the post-mortem document.

# On-call rotation

On-call rotates weekly, Monday 10am to the following Monday 10am.
Handoff is a 15-minute sync including any open issues from the week.
Primary and secondary rotate separately so no one is primary and
secondary in the same week. Swap requests go through the scheduling
tool with at least 24 hours notice.
